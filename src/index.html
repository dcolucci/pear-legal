About

Olivia Jahn is an accomplished healthcare attorney and executive leader with extensive experience navigating the legal, regulatory, and operational complexities of the health tech industry. Currently serving as General Counsel at Thirty Madison, Olivia leads a multidisciplinary team overseeing legal, compliance, and privacy functions at a direct-to-consumer healthcare company.

Before Thirty Madison, Olivia held legal roles at Flatiron Health and top-tier law firms McDermott Will & Emery and Hogan Lovells. She holds a J.D. and a Graduate Certificate in Health Policy from The George Washington University, and a B.S.B.A. in Finance and Healthcare Management from Washington University in St. Louis.

Previously, she sat on the Junior Advisory Board of Her Justice, a non-profit organization providing pro bono legal services to women living in poverty in NYC.
